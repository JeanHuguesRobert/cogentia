# RT-TTP-001 — Tony Toma / Minesteggio

## Purpose

Reconstruct retrospectively whether the Trace Treatment Packet profile would have represented useful independent cognitive work without adding material bureaucracy.

## Raw trace

A 2026 Corse Net Infos article identified a recently deceased Cortenais mandolinist as **Tony Toma**. The Principal recognized this as the missing surname of the person previously remembered only as "Tony", who had supplied an oral hypothesis about the possible etymology of Minesteggio.

Raw public trace:

- Mario Grazi, *Notte di a Mandulina in Corti : Une 8e édition riche en émotions*, Corse Net Infos, 2 August 2026.

## Independent cognitive question

> Is this public Tony Toma sufficiently identified as the person remembered by the Principal, and what does recovering the name change in the Corpus?

This question justified independent treatment because it required corroboration, epistemic qualification, propagation across an active Living Publication, and explicit closure while leaving the etymology itself open.

## Reconstructed treatment

```yaml
packet_kind: trace-treatment
trace:
  ref: "Corse Net Infos, 2026-08-02"
question: "Does recovering 'Tony Toma' restore provenance for the Minesteggio oral hypothesis?"
qualification:
  observed:
    - "Corse Net Infos publicly names Tony Toma as a Cortenais mandolinist."
  reported:
    - "The Principal identifies Antoine 'Tony' Toma as the person who communicated the etymological hypothesis."
  corroborated_identity:
    - "public Toulouse academic traces"
    - "public death notice for Antoine (Tony) Toma"
  not_established:
    - "the etymology itself"
disposition: integrate_provenance
propagation:
  - "Minesteggio research dossier"
  - "Rise & Fall provenance note"
  - "Rise & Fall Book projection"
  - "Rise & Fall Magazine projection"
  - "Rise & Fall site / machine-readable guide surface"
closure:
  status: closed
  remaining_open:
    - "historical-linguistic validity of the Minesteggio etymology"
```

## What the Packet would have protected

1. **Identity vs hypothesis** — public sources corroborate the identity; they do not prove the etymology.
2. **Oral provenance** — the attribution of the hypothesis to Tony Toma remains the Principal's direct testimony.
3. **Bounded closure** — provenance recovery can close while linguistic investigation remains open.
4. **Propagation accounting** — the change affected several derived surfaces and was not merely a local correction.

## Friction assessment

The treatment already occurred through ordinary conversational and GitHub work. A lightweight Packet would mainly have made the work frontier explicit.

Retrospective judgment:

```text
additional useful structure: low
expected resumability gain: high
expected bureaucracy: low if represented by reference
```

## Result

**PASS as a motivating retrospective case.**

This does not validate a universal schema. It supports the narrower claim that a materially consequential trace treatment can be represented as a Cognitive Packet without requiring the raw trace itself to become a Packet.

## Residual question

Could the same profile remain lightweight when the trace is not documentary, when several hypotheses remain live, and when the work cannot be closed in one session? RT-TTP-003 is intended to test that boundary.
