# Evolutionary Learning Computer — Research Programme

## Abstract

*The Network is the Learning Computer* defines a network that learns when the return and assimilation of Cognitive Packet journeys change how future packets are produced, routed, composed or processed. This note asks a more discriminating research question: **Does bounded, governed variation and transmission across populations of Cognitive Packet-backed methods improve collective capability, resilience and discovery over equally resourced single-lineage or last-state-only baselines?** The candidate mechanism joins Cognitive Packet Closure, provenance, typed forks, independently evaluated variants, RAIX capability composition, stigmergic reinforcement, FractaMemory/FractaCache cooling, glacial preservation and present-time revalidation. It is neither an assertion of biological evolution nor a new COP object.

## Research question and scope

The level of analysis matters. A packet is a unit of *continuable work*, not a gene or an agent. A population of packet-backed variants is an analytical collection, not a new normative entity. A learning ecosystem comprises handlers, evaluation regimes, stores, localities and policies. Adaptation at one level does not prove adaptation at the next.

The established Learning Computer proposition already distinguishes storage from consequential learning:

```text
packet → journey → return to Ithaca → assimilation → changed future journeys
```

Our candidate extension:

```text
several attributable variations
→ comparable experiments and Reality Responses
→ governed retention, recombination or cooling
→ reproducible methods in successor agents
→ changed network capability distribution
→ renewed exploration under current conditions
```

An unchanged packet identity cannot be taken as a lineage edge; a COP fork cannot be assumed to be an evolutionary mutation; frequency of propagation must not be equated with truth or fitness.

## State of the art: distinct but complementary traditions

1. **Evolutionary epistemology** (Donald T. Campbell; Karl Popper): variation, criticism and selective retention as lenses on knowledge growth; not necessarily biological reproduction.
2. **Cultural evolution and dual inheritance** (Cavalli-Sforza & Feldman; Boyd & Richerson): vertical, horizontal and oblique transmission; biased, imperfect acquisition; cultural transmission need not resemble genes.
3. **Quality-diversity / MAP-Elites** (Mouret & Clune): maintain distinct high-performing solutions in behaviorally defined niches rather than one winner.
4. **Open-ended agent evolution:** [Darwin Gödel Machine (2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.22954) evolves code-modifying agents with empirical benchmark feedback and a growing archive. The object evolving is a coding agent; the packet analogy is ours, not a claim by the authors.
5. **LLM-mediated evolutionary optimization:** [AlphaEvolve (2025)](https://deepmind.google/blog/alphaevolve-a-gemini-powered-coding-agent-for-designing-advanced-algorithms/) combines LLM proposals, executable verifiers and an evolutionary archive. [DeepMind's May 2026 update](https://deepmind.google/blog/alphaevolve-impact/) reports concrete cache-replacement and storage-compaction applications. Those successes occur in domains where objective evaluation is feasible; transfer to epistemic judgment needs independent validation.
6. **Experimental reproducibility and negative transfer:** external memory and replay do not automatically guarantee useful adaptation; test transfer on independent cases rather than only optimizing a single task.

These works are friends and baselines, not novelty foils. This initial bibliography is selective and must be expanded with exact editions, evaluation designs and limitations before academic submission.

## Hypotheses, competing explanations and falsifiers

**H1 — Reproducible inheritance.** An authorized cold successor can reproduce a useful learned method from packet references and assimilated traces without predecessor-private session memory. *Null:* no improvement over a last-result-only summary. *Falsifier:* knowledge transmission fails under cold replacement or depends on hidden state.

**H2 — Diversity adds option value.** Maintaining a bounded set of materially distinct variants improves performance on changed or unfamiliar tasks, at equal total budget, over retaining only the latest winner. *Null:* diversity imposes storage/evaluation overhead without robust gain. *Falsifier:* no consequence-adjusted benefit after equalizing resources.

**H3 — Glacial refugia enable recovery.** Cooling nonwinning variants with a documented recovery path increases adaptability after distribution shift. *Null:* archived variants are never worth the restoration cost. *Falsifier:* reinvoked candidates fail present-time validation or never outperform fresh exploration.

**H4 — Governed selection outperforms stigmergic popularity.** Reality-verified, provenance-aware selection produces fewer wrong promotions than reuse frequency alone. *Null:* extra review adds latency but no improvement. *Falsifier:* critical selection does not reduce held-out error or capture.

**H5 — RAIX composition learns at network level.** Assimilated histories of temporary capability arrays improve future composition under matched budgets. *Null:* ordinary stateless routing is sufficient. *Falsifier:* no improvement on cold-handler held-out workloads.

**H6 — Evolvable evaluators require independent anchors.** Coevolving evaluation policies can improve adaptation but increase Goodhart risk; fixed external held-out anchors detect misleading apparent progress. *Null:* evaluator drift does not materially alter outcomes. *Falsifier:* proposed checks fail to detect deliberately contaminated metrics.

## Experimental design

**Unit:** one bounded, non-consequential coding or corpus-maintenance task family with explicit acceptance conditions. Use a reproducible baseline, frozen held-out tasks and resource budgets. Run independent repeated trials, record random seeds and predeclare primary metrics. Do not tune on held-out cases.

**Arms:** A: fixed baseline method; B: single-lineage iterative improvement; C: packet-backed diversity with governed selection; D: C plus glacial archives and present-time rehydration. All arms share the same compute/token, elapsed-time and human-attention budget envelope, or report normalized results and unequal-resource sensitivity.

**Measurements:** held-out quality; transfer after handler substitution; genealogy fidelity; diversity and niche coverage; adaptation after task distribution shift; recovery cost; stale-fact acceptance; privacy or mandate violations; financial, energy, bandwidth, token and attention costs. Distinguish transport/test execution success from the quality of its outcome.

**Controls:** ablate provenance, revalidation, diversity and policy separately; insert deliberately stale information, evaluator drift, duplicated traces and noisy popularity signals. Repeat after replacing the original handler with an admissible cold handler. Preserve evidence of negative results.

**Prediction discipline:** claim improvement only within measured conditions; a successful closed-loop demonstration is not proof of autonomous open-ended evolution. Evolution of evaluators or objectives needs fresh authorization and explicit comparisons, never self-issued mandates.

## Why this is not merely Darwinian selection

- Cognitive Packet continuation is not faithful genetic replication.
- Variation may be deliberate, recombinative, critical or guided by human judgment.
- Acquired cognitive improvements can be transmitted horizontally through explicit corpus artifacts, unlike a simplistic hereditary-gene analogy.
- Selection is multidimensional and governed: factual soundness, reproducibility, consequences, sovereignty, diversity and cost.
- A successful heuristic in one locality need not be optimal elsewhere.
- The environment and evaluator may coevolve; versions and provenance must be preserved.
- Dormant or discarded operational paths can remain historical evidence; active evaporation is not authorized erasure.

## Implications for packet semantics and the Learning Computer thesis

No new envelope field or COP wire type is justified at this stage. Instead, use existing lineage, references, typed forks, evaluations, receipts, return/Ithaca and reconstruction mechanisms to create optional, reconstructible *population projections*. An evolution experiment is a composition of normal packets plus evaluation policy, not a new sovereign packet category.

The strongest *candidate* extension to the Learning Computer thesis is **network-level capacity evolution**: not only do future journeys change; the distribution, diversity and composability of available methods and capabilities also change through evaluated experience. Whether this is distinct from ordinary cumulative learning must be empirically discriminated.

The experiment should also compare the simplest existing COP + Git + corpus-store implementation against new infrastructure: **no separate Evolutionary Computer, FractaX runtime or genetic packet schema before measured necessity**.
