## Established decisions

### 1. The present has no privilege over The Possible

The anti-presentist rule is constitutional in spirit but operational in form.

```text
unknown          != impossible
unsupported      != impossible
unrepresented    != impossible
unfamiliar       != impossible
unavailable_now  != impossible
```

A claim explicitly marked `impossible` should carry a basis appropriate to its scope. Prefer scoped forms such as `impossible_under_current_regime` when the constraint is contingent.

### 2. Open-Possible is a mechanism, not an exhortation

The working discipline is:

```text
FRAME
CHALLENGE
PRESERVE
OPEN
BOOST
TEST
```

The point is not to force novelty. `none` and `none_identified` are valid. The mechanism must make premature closure visible and leave a trace.

### 3. Booster Principle

A **Booster** is a relatively small intervention that can unlock, reveal, redirect, or mobilize a disproportionately large latent potentiality in a sufficiently fertile substrate.

Important distinctions:

```text
Fertile Ground = property/configuration of the substrate
Booster        = intervention leveraging that substrate
Potentics      = study of potentiality, inhibitors, accessibility and actualization
```

The doctrine does not claim that a Booster always exists. The operational obligation is to search for one before adding substantial force, especially when a candidate experiment is cheap, bounded and reversible.

### 4. Optimistic Locking applies to exploration itself

Do not try to prevent every epistemic mistake before action. Prefer small, traceable, reversible experiments when they can discriminate between live hypotheses.

Canonical compression:

```text
Open.
Try small.
Let Reality answer.
Keep the trace.
Correct.
```

### 5. Epistemic assimilation and salience

A major failure mode was stabilized separately:

```text
knowledge represented != knowledge currently salient
coherence after revelation != discovery before revelation
```

When a new observation `x` is projected into a current conceptual basis `B`:

\[
x = P_B(x) + r
\]

premature assimilation treats the projection as the whole and discards residual `r`. The Open-Possible discipline preserves significant persistent residue long enough to test whether the frame, rather than the phenomenon, needs revision.

The phrase **epistemic gravity of the known** may describe this attraction informally, but MUST NOT be conflated with Cogentia's existing **Informational Gravity**, which is a routing relation between cognitive packets and mobilizable capacities.

### 6. Operational reality and the "as if" regime

A representation may be ontologically uncertain but operationally real.

```text
ontological uncertainty
+ need to act
-> operational hypothesis/regime
+ trace
+ contestability
+ revisability
```

Canonical formulas:

> **Agnosticisme ontologique, engagement opérationnel.**

> **Agir comme si, sans oublier le « si ».**

Responsibility can therefore be treated as an operational and procedural regime without claiming to have solved the metaphysics of free will.

### 7. Witness, actor, experimenter

The possibilist actor is not merely an observer.

```text
territory
-> traces
-> map
-> decision
-> action
-> transformed territory
-> new traces
-> corrected map
```

When action is selected to obtain a response from Reality, the witness-actor becomes an experimenter.

### 8. Reflexive maps

A map may enter the territory causally once actors observe and act on it.

> **A map of The Possible, once observed by an actor capable of acting upon it, becomes part of The Possible it maps.**

This matters for predictions, recommendation systems, markets, polling, risk scores, Potentiality Maps, Digital Twins, and self-fulfilling or self-defeating prophecies.

Procedural rule:

> **A representation should not transform the territory and then invoke that transformation as unqualified evidence of its own predictive truth.**

### 9. Joy, capability and play

The corpus already had **The Joyful Exploration of the Possible** and play as serious method. The new conceptual proposal is:

> **La joie est l'expérience vécue de l'accroissement du champ des possibles.**

Treat this as a conceptual proposition, not a universal psychological law.

The emphasis is transition-sensitive:

```text
I could not
-> transition
-> now I can
-> opening
-> possible joy
```

Do not collapse joy into `pleasure`, `reward`, `utility`, or `reinforcement` merely because those categories are computationally familiar.

Play is treated as temporary operationalization of a possible:

> **Jouer, c'est donner temporairement une réalité opérationnelle à un possible afin de découvrir ce qu'il permet.**

Continuum:

```text
fiction
-> play
-> simulation
-> serious game
-> prototype
-> experiment
-> institution
-> world action
```
