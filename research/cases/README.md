# Institutional Friction Cases

This directory contains structured cases used to study administrative burden, institutional friction, Intent–Reality Gaps, and Exemplar Tests.

Canonical doctrine:

- `../administrative_burden_and_exemplar_tests.md`
- `../institutional_friction_cases.schema.yaml`

Methodological rule:

> Facts and interpretations must remain distinct.

A case may begin with sparse facts and explicit hypotheses. Missing evidence must remain visibly missing rather than being inferred into the factual layer.

Initial cases:

- `FR-INPI-001-digipees.yaml` — free trademark prior-art assistance tested on Digipees;
- `FR-EIDAS-001-open-timestamp-traces.yaml` — open high-quality timestamp traces versus qualified timestamp services.

An individual administrative interaction may provide evidence for a case, but this registry is distinct from any interaction log: interactions are observations; cases are structured objects for comparison, interpretation, and testing.
