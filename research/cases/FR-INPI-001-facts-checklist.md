# FR-INPI-001 — Fact collection checklist

This checklist supports the Digipees Exemplar without mixing observations and interpretations.

## Public-service facts to capture

- current INPI mission statements relevant to dissemination of industrial-property information;
- current free trademark-search capabilities and documented limitations;
- current paid similarity-search offerings and tariffs;
- current INPI trademark API/open-data scope, access conditions, update frequency, fields, and limits;
- filing costs and relevant Nice-class mechanics;
- e-Soleau role, cost, retention period, and explicit statement that it does not create trademark rights;
- other relevant French/EU public services if they materially affect the comparison.

## Exemplar measurements

For each test sign, including `Digipees`:

- query and intended uses;
- candidate Nice classes;
- free public search results;
- candidates produced by deterministic similarity;
- candidates added or rejected by external judgment;
- useful false positives / false negatives discovered by review;
- elapsed time;
- user actions;
- service-side compute and marginal cost;
- external-agent work supplied by the user;
- limitations and unresolved legal questions.

## Interpretation discipline

Do not encode as facts:

- negligence;
- double discourse;
- institutional hypocrisy;
- protection of commercial rents;
- capture;
- deliberate obstruction.

These remain hypotheses in the case record and must point to supporting facts before their status is raised.
